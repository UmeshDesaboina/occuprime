What We Do

End-to-end occupational health, under one roof

From onsite clinics to emergency fleets and certification audits, we handle every layer of workplace health and safety so your team can focus on production.

Turnkey OHC Management

We design, set up, staff and operate your onsite Occupational Health Center with qualified medical officers and nursing personnel.

  • Layout, equipment and pharmacy setup
  • Doctors, nurses and paramedics on roster
  • Digital health records and MIS reports

Statutory Medical Checkups

Pre-employment, periodic and exit health examinations carried out as required under factory acts and labour laws.

  • Audiometry, spirometry, vision and X-ray
  • Form 32 / 33 and certification support
  • Trend analytics for workforce health

FMO Advisory

Expert Factory Medical Officer guidance on industrial hygiene, occupational disease surveillance and toxicological risk.

  • Hazard and exposure assessment
  • Occupational disease surveillance
  • Regulator and inspector liaison

Ambulance Fleet Operations

Fully equipped industrial ambulances with trained EMTs for rapid response across your plant and campus.

  • BLS / ALS equipped vehicles
  • Certified EMTs and drivers
  • Mock drills and response protocols

EHS & ISO 45001 Audits

Rigorous internal audits of your OH&S management system to cut operational risk and get certification-ready.

  • Gap analysis against ISO 45001
  • Corrective action tracking
  • Management review reports
